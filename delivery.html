Questions

Delivery FAQ

Can I open the parcel before paying cash? +

Yes. Ask the rider to wait while you open the box and check the seal and bottle. Pay only when you are happy.

What if the bottle arrives broken? +

Send us a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery and we will send a new bottle at no cost.

Do you deliver outside Pakistan? +

Not yet. International shipping to the UAE and UK is planned for 2027.

Can I change my address after ordering? +

Yes, as long as the order has not left our atelier. Message us with your order number and the new address.

Can someone else receive the parcel? +

Yes. Any adult at the address can receive it. For cash on delivery, they will need to pay the rider.
